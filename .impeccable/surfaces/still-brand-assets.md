# Still publication assets

Mode: Read. Narrow extension of the established diary identity for a blog cover and browser favicon; no new visual world or model-generated imagery.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A small courier in a spacious world. Avoid AI-brain imagery, hardware glamour and fake terminal screenshots.

OWN-WORLD: Existing cool ground, blue-gray ink, Georgia title and restrained line drawing. The favicon reduces the messenger bag to a high-contrast, small-size mark rather than shrinking a detailed person.

STORY: Identify Still as a courier's diary written locally by Gemma. Artwork is illustrative, not a screenshot or claim about a recorded event.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 1200 × 630 canvas. Title and short factual description on the left; the established courier approaching an entryway on the right, joined by a ground line. Generous crop-safe margins. Browser mark must remain recognizable at 16 and 32 pixels.

FORM: Precisely scoped brand-asset extension, inheriting the viewer's seed:b310af4a7baddb8f and approved courier vocabulary. SVG is the editable source; the PNG is a direct raster export, with source provenance embedded.
